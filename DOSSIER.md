### Recovery Protocol

**Recovery Triggers (Auto-Deload):**
- HRV ↓ > 20% vs baseline 7d → sostituire sessione hard con recovery, monitorare 24h
- RHR ↑ ≥ 5 bpm vs baseline → deload, no intensità quel giorno
- Sleep quality POOR per 3+ giorni consecutivi → flag amber→red, ridurre volume 20%
- Feel ≥ 4 → sessione automaticamente declassata a recovery
- Due o più trigger simultanei → giorno di riposo completo, no training

**Feel Scale:**
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Excellent (fully recovered) |
| 2 | Good (normal fatigue) |
| 3 | Moderate (manageable tiredness) |
| 4 | Fatigued (reduced readiness, deload trigger) |
| 5 | Exhausted (complete rest required) |
